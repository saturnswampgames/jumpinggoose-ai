JUMPINGGOOSE Agency Text Intelligence

Single n8n workflow for discovery, strategy, communication, social content, copy, general brand guidance, memory, and optional web research. Visual and image-generation work is intentionally excluded.

35 nodes 9 wait nodes 9 HTTP request nodes Single workflow
